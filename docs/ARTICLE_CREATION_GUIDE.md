# Article Creation and Submission Guide

A step-by-step guide for authors to create, edit, and submit articles for publication.

## Quick Start Checklist

Before you begin writing:
- [ ] You have author access to the Admin Dashboard
- [ ] You've read the content guidelines
- [ ] You have your topic and research ready
- [ ] You have any images or media prepared (under 2MB each)

## Step-by-Step Article Creation

### Step 1: Access the Article Creator

1. **Login** to your account
2. **Navigate** to Admin Dashboard
3. **Click** "Articles" in the left sidebar
4. **Click** "New Article" button (top right)

### Step 2: Basic Article Information

**Article Title**
- Keep it clear and engaging
- 50-80 characters works best
- Include keywords readers might search for
- Examples:
  - ✅ "How Solar Panels Work: A Kid's Guide to Clean Energy"
  - ❌ "Solar Stuff"

**Category Selection**
Choose the most appropriate category:
- **Headliners**: Breaking news and current events
- **Debates**: Discussion topics with multiple viewpoints
- **Learning**: Educational content and tutorials
- **Neighborhood**: Local community news and events
- **School News**: School-related updates and information
- **Spice It Up**: Entertainment, fun facts, and lighter content
- **Storyboard**: Creative writing, stories, and serialized content

**Featured Image**
- Upload a compelling image that represents your article
- Recommended size: 1200x630 pixels
- File formats: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP
- Maximum size: 2MB
- Ensure you have rights to use the image

### Step 3: Writing Your Article

**Content Editor Features**
The rich text editor includes:
- **Formatting**: Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough
- **Headings**: H1, H2, H3 for structure
- **Lists**: Bulleted and numbered lists
- **Links**: Internal and external links
- **Images**: Inline image insertion
- **Quotes**: Block quotes for emphasis
- **Code**: Code blocks for technical content

**Writing Best Practices**

**Structure Your Content:**
```
1. Engaging introduction (hook the reader)
2. Main content with clear headings
3. Supporting details and examples
4. Conclusion that summarizes key points
```

**Writing Style:**
- Use clear, simple language appropriate for young readers
- Break up long paragraphs (3-4 sentences max)
- Include subheadings every 200-300 words
- Use active voice when possible
- Add examples and analogies to explain complex topics

**Content Length Guidelines:**
- **Minimum**: 200 words (required)
- **Sweet spot**: 500-1,500 words
- **Maximum**: 5,000 words
- **Reading time**: Aim for 3-8 minutes

### Step 4: Adding Media and Enhancements

**Images Within Articles**
- Click the image icon in the editor toolbar
- Upload images or select from media library
- Add alt text for accessibility
- Position images to break up text blocks

**Links and References**
- Link to credible sources for facts and statistics
- Use descriptive link text (not "click here")
- Open external links in new tabs
- Credit sources at the end of your article

**Formatting for Readability**
- Use bullet points for lists
- Bold important terms or concepts
- Use block quotes for emphasis
- Add line breaks between sections

### Step 5: Article Settings and Metadata

**Tags**
- Add 3-5 relevant tags
- Use specific, searchable terms
- Examples: "science", "environment", "kids", "education"
- Help readers find your content

**Meta Description**
- Write a 150-160 character summary
- Include your main keyword
- Make it compelling to encourage clicks
- This appears in search results

**Reading Level**
- The system automatically calculates reading level
- Aim for grade 6-8 level for accessibility
- Use shorter sentences and common words

**Publication Settings**
- **Publish immediately**: Article goes live after approval
- **Schedule for later**: Set a future publication date
- **Save as draft**: Keep working on it later

### Step 6: Review Before Submission

**Content Review Checklist**
- [ ] Title is clear and engaging
- [ ] Content is well-structured with headings
- [ ] All facts are accurate and sourced
- [ ] Images have alt text and proper attribution
- [ ] Links work and open appropriately
- [ ] Grammar and spelling are correct
- [ ] Content is appropriate for target audience
- [ ] Article meets minimum word count (200 words)

**Technical Review Checklist**
- [ ] Featured image is uploaded and displays correctly
- [ ] All inline images load properly
- [ ] Tags are relevant and specific
- [ ] Meta description is compelling and accurate
- [ ] Category selection is appropriate

### Step 7: Submit for Review

**Submission Options**

**Save as Draft**
- Saves your work without submitting
- You can continue editing later
- Not visible to reviewers or readers
- No time limit on drafts

**Submit for Review**
- Sends article to moderation queue
- Cannot edit while under review
- Review typically takes 1-3 business days
- You'll be notified of the decision

**What Happens During Review**

**Reviewers Check For:**
- Content quality and accuracy
- Age-appropriate language and themes
- Proper grammar and formatting
- Compliance with community guidelines
- Factual accuracy and source credibility

**Possible Outcomes:**
1. **Approved**: Article is published automatically
2. **Needs Changes**: You'll receive feedback and can resubmit
3. **Rejected**: Article won't be published (with explanation)

## After Submission

### Tracking Your Article Status

**Status Types:**
- **Draft**: Saved but not submitted
- **Pending Review**: Submitted and awaiting approval
- **Needs Changes**: Requires modifications before approval
- **Approved**: Published and live on the site
- **Rejected**: Not approved for publication

**Where to Check Status:**
1. Go to Articles page in your dashboard
2. Look for status indicators next to your articles
3. Click on an article to see detailed status information

### Responding to Reviewer Feedback

**If Changes Are Requested:**
1. Read the feedback carefully
2. Make the requested changes
3. Resubmit for review
4. Add a note explaining what you changed

**If Your Article is Rejected:**
1. Review the rejection reason
2. Consider if you can address the concerns
3. Create a new version or choose a different topic
4. Contact support if you need clarification

### After Publication

**Promoting Your Article:**
- Share on social media (if appropriate)
- Respond to reader comments
- Monitor analytics to see how it performs
- Consider writing follow-up articles on popular topics

**Monitoring Performance:**
- Check view counts and engagement
- Read and respond to comments
- Use analytics to understand your audience
- Learn from high-performing articles

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Content Mistakes
- **Too short**: Articles under 200 words lack depth
- **Too long**: Articles over 2,000 words may lose readers
- **No structure**: Wall of text without headings or breaks
- **Unclear title**: Vague or clickbait titles
- **No sources**: Claims without credible references

### Technical Mistakes
- **Large images**: Files over 2MB won't upload
- **Broken links**: Always test links before submitting
- **Missing alt text**: Images need descriptions for accessibility
- **Wrong category**: Misplaced articles confuse readers
- **No tags**: Articles without tags are harder to find

### Submission Mistakes
- **Rushing review**: Submit only when truly ready
- **Ignoring feedback**: Not addressing reviewer comments
- **Duplicate content**: Submitting the same article multiple times
- **Wrong format**: Not using the rich text editor properly

## Tips for Success

### Writing Tips
- **Start with an outline** to organize your thoughts
- **Write for your audience** - remember you're writing for young readers
- **Use examples** to illustrate complex concepts
- **Tell stories** to make content more engaging
- **End with action** - what should readers do next?

### Efficiency Tips
- **Prepare content offline** in a word processor first
- **Batch similar tasks** (write multiple articles, then edit all)
- **Create templates** for recurring article types
- **Keep a list of article ideas** for when inspiration strikes
- **Set writing schedules** to maintain consistency

### Quality Tips
- **Read your article aloud** to catch awkward phrasing
- **Get feedback** from friends or family before submitting
- **Research thoroughly** and cite credible sources
- **Edit ruthlessly** - remove unnecessary words
- **Focus on value** - what will readers learn or gain?

## Getting Help

### During Article Creation
- **Save frequently** to avoid losing work
- **Use the preview feature** to see how your article will look
- **Contact support** if you encounter technical issues
- **Ask for help** with research or fact-checking

### If You're Stuck
- **Review successful articles** by other authors for inspiration
- **Check the FAQ** for common questions
- **Contact other authors** for advice
- **Take breaks** - sometimes stepping away helps

### Technical Support
- **Browser issues**: Try a different browser or clear cache
- **Upload problems**: Check file size and format
- **Editor problems**: Refresh the page and try again
- **Lost work**: Check drafts folder for auto-saved content

## Ready to Start Writing?

Now that you understand the process:

1. **Choose your topic** - something you're passionate about
2. **Do your research** - gather facts and sources
3. **Create an outline** - organize your thoughts
4. **Write your first draft** - don't worry about perfection
5. **Edit and refine** - make it the best it can be
