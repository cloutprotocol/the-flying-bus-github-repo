## Getting Started

### What is an Author Role?

As an author, you have special privileges that allow you to:
- Create and publish articles
- Edit your own content
- Submit articles for review
- View analytics for your content
- Manage comments on your articles

### Accessing the Author Dashboard

1. **Login** to your account using your registered email and password
2. **Navigate** to the Admin Dashboard (you'll see this option in your navigation menu)
3. **Dashboard Access**: You'll automatically be taken to your author-specific dashboard

> **Note**: If you don't see the Admin Dashboard option, please contact an administrator to verify your author role has been properly assigned.

## Author Dashboard Overview

Your author dashboard provides a simplified view of the admin interface, showing only the features relevant to content creation and management.

### Available Features

✅ **Features You Can Access:**
- **Dashboard**: Overview of your content performance
- **Articles**: Create, edit, and manage your articles
- **Media**: Upload and manage images and files
- **Comments**: Moderate comments on your articles

❌ **Features You Cannot Access:**
- **Content Review**: Reserved for moderators and admins
- **Users**: User management is admin-only
- **Invitations**: Invitation management is admin-only
- **Settings**: System settings are admin-only

### Dashboard Metrics

Your dashboard shows metrics specific to your content:

- **Your Articles**: Total number of articles you've created
- **Your Article Views**: Total views across all your articles
- **Your Comments**: Comments received on your articles
- **Articles in Review**: Number of articles currently pending approval
- **Published Articles**: Number of your articles that are live

## Creating Articles

### Step-by-Step Article Creation

1. **Navigate to Articles**
   - Click "Articles" in the left navigation menu
   - Click the "New Article" button (top right)

2. **Fill in Article Details**
   - **Title**: Write a compelling, descriptive title
   - **Category**: Select the appropriate category (Headliners, Learning, etc.)
   - **Content**: Write your article using the rich text editor
   - **Featured Image**: Upload an image to represent your article

3. **Article Settings**
   - **Tags**: Add relevant tags to help readers find your content
   - **Meta Description**: Write a brief description for SEO
   - **Reading Time**: This is calculated automatically

4. **Save and Submit**
   - **Save as Draft**: Save your work without submitting for review
   - **Submit for Review**: Send your article to moderators for approval

### Content Guidelines

**Writing Best Practices:**
- Keep content appropriate for children and young readers
- Use clear, engaging language
- Include relevant images and media
- Structure content with headings and bullet points
- Fact-check all information before submitting

**Technical Requirements:**
- Minimum 200 words for article body
- Maximum 5,000 words per article
- Images should be under 2MB each
- Supported image formats: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP

## Managing Your Articles

### Article List View

In the Articles section, you'll see:
- **All Articles**: Complete list of articles on the platform
- **Your Articles**: Filter to show only articles you've created
- **Status Indicators**: Visual indicators showing article status

### Editing Your Articles

1. **Find Your Article**: Use the filter or search to locate your article
2. **Click Edit**: Only articles you created will have an "Edit" button
3. **Make Changes**: Edit content, title, or settings as needed
4. **Resubmit**: After editing, articles return to "Pending Review" status

### Article Actions

For your own articles, you can:
- ✏️ **Edit**: Modify content and settings
- 👁️ **Preview**: See how the article will look when published
- 📊 **View Stats**: See performance metrics
- 🗑️ **Delete**: Remove the article (if not yet published)

For other authors' articles, you can only:
- 👁️ **View**: Read the published content
- 📊 **View Public Stats**: See basic performance metrics

## Article Review Process

### How the Review Process Works

1. **Submit for Review**: When you submit an article, it gets "Pending Review" status
2. **Moderator Review**: A moderator or admin reviews your content
3. **Approval Decision**: The reviewer can approve, request changes, or reject
4. **Publication**: Approved articles are automatically published
5. **Feedback**: You'll receive feedback if changes are requested

### Review Timeline

- **Standard Review**: 1-3 business days
- **Complex Articles**: Up to 5 business days
- **Urgent Content**: Contact an admin for expedited review

### What Reviewers Look For

**Content Quality:**
- Accuracy of information
- Appropriate language and tone
- Proper grammar and spelling
- Engaging and well-structured content

**Platform Guidelines:**
- Age-appropriate content
- No inappropriate language or themes
- Proper attribution of sources
- Compliance with community standards

## Understanding Article Status

### Status Types

| Status | Description | What You Can Do |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Draft** | Article saved but not submitted | Edit, delete, or submit for review |
| **Pending Review** | Submitted and awaiting approval | Wait for review, view status |
| **Approved** | Approved and published | View analytics, edit (triggers new review) |
| **Needs Changes** | Requires modifications before approval | Edit based on feedback, resubmit |
| **Rejected** | Not approved for publication | Review feedback, create new version |

### Status Notifications

You'll be notified when:
- Your article is approved and published
- Changes are requested
- Your article is rejected (with feedback)
- Your article receives significant engagement

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### "I can't see the Admin Dashboard option"

**Possible Causes:**
- Your author role hasn't been assigned yet
- You're not logged in
- Browser cache issues

**Solutions:**
1. Log out and log back in
2. Clear your browser cache
3. Contact an administrator to verify your role

#### "My article isn't showing up after approval"

**Possible Causes:**
- Article is scheduled for future publication
- Caching delay
- Technical issue

**Solutions:**
1. Wait 5-10 minutes for cache refresh
2. Check if article has a future publish date
3. Contact support if issue persists

#### "I can't edit my own article"

**Possible Causes:**
- Article is currently being reviewed
- Technical permissions issue
- Browser compatibility issue

**Solutions:**
1. Wait if article is in review
2. Try a different browser
3. Contact support for permission issues

#### "My images won't upload"

**Possible Causes:**
- File size too large (over 2MB)
- Unsupported file format
- Network connectivity issues

**Solutions:**
1. Compress images to under 2MB
2. Use JPG, PNG, GIF, or WebP formats
3. Check your internet connection
4. Try uploading one image at a time

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Account and Access

**Q: How do I know if I have author privileges?**
A: If you can see the "Admin Dashboard" option in your navigation menu and can create articles, you have author privileges.

**Q: Can I invite other authors?**
A: No, only administrators can send invitations. If you know someone who should be an author, contact an admin.

**Q: Can I change my role from author to admin?**
A: Role changes must be made by existing administrators. Contact support if you need a role change.

### Content Creation

**Q: How many articles can I create?**
A: There's no limit on the number of articles you can create.

**Q: Can I schedule articles for future publication?**
A: Yes, you can set a future publish date when creating or editing articles.

**Q: Can I collaborate with other authors on articles?**
A: Currently, each article can only have one author. For collaborative content, one author should create the article and credit others in the content.

**Q: What happens if I delete a published article?**
A: Deleting published articles removes them from the site immediately. This action cannot be undone, so use carefully.

### Review Process

**Q: Why was my article rejected?**
A: Common reasons include inappropriate content, factual errors, poor quality, or guideline violations. Check the feedback provided with the rejection.

**Q: Can I appeal a rejection decision?**
A: Yes, contact an administrator to discuss the rejection and potentially appeal the decision.

**Q: How can I speed up the review process?**
A: Ensure your content follows all guidelines, is well-written, and includes proper formatting. High-quality submissions are typically approved faster.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Why can't I see all the admin features?**
A: Authors have limited access to admin features. You can only access article management, media uploads, and your own analytics.

**Q: Can I see other authors' unpublished articles?**
A: No, you can only see and edit your own articles. Published articles by other authors are visible to everyone.

**Q: How do I backup my articles?**
A: You can copy your article content to save locally. The platform automatically saves all your work.
