# Parent Guide: Invitation Claim Process

## Welcome to The Flying Bus!

This guide will help you understand how to claim your child's invitation to become an author on The Flying Bus platform.

## What is The Flying Bus?

The Flying Bus is a safe, educational news platform where children can write, publish, and share news articles. Our platform is designed specifically for young journalists and readers, with comprehensive safety measures and educational support.

## The Invitation Process

### Step 1: Invitation Request Submitted
You've already completed this step by submitting an invitation request for your child to become an author on our platform.

### Step 2: Admin Review
Our team reviews each invitation request to ensure the safety and quality of our author community. This process typically takes 3-5 business days.

### Step 3: Approval Notification
If your child's invitation is approved, you'll receive an email notification with:
- Confirmation of approval
- A unique invitation link
- Instructions for account setup
- Platform guidelines and safety information

### Step 4: Account Creation or Upgrade

#### If you already have an account:
1. Click the invitation link in your approval email
2. Sign in to your existing account
3. Your account will automatically be upgraded to author privileges
4. You'll receive a welcome email with next steps

#### If you don't have an account:
1. Click the invitation link in your approval email
2. Fill out the account creation form with:
   - Your email address (must match the invitation request)
   - A secure password
   - Basic profile information
3. Verify your email address
4. Your new account will have author privileges automatically

### Step 5: Welcome and Onboarding
After successful account setup, you'll receive:
- A welcome email with platform guidelines
- Information about child safety measures
- Getting started guide for young authors
- Contact information for support

## Important Information

### Security and Safety
- **Invitation links are unique and expire after 30 days**
- Only use the official invitation link sent to your email
- Never share your invitation link with others
- All content on the platform is moderated for safety

### Account Requirements
- You must use the same email address that was used for the invitation request
- Passwords must be secure (at least 8 characters with mixed case and numbers)
- Profile information should be accurate for safety purposes

### Platform Guidelines
- All articles must be appropriate for children
- Content is reviewed before publication
- We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for inappropriate content
- Parents can monitor their child's activity through the platform

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### "Invalid or expired invitation link"
- **Cause**: The invitation link has expired (after 30 days) or has already been used
- **Solution**: Contact our support team at support@theflyingbus.com to request a new invitation link

#### "Email address doesn't match invitation request"
- **Cause**: You're trying to use a different email address than the one used for the invitation request
- **Solution**: Use the exact email address that was provided in the original invitation request

#### "Account already exists"
- **Cause**: An account with your email address already exists
- **Solution**: Use the "Sign In" option instead of creating a new account. Your existing account will be upgraded automatically.

#### "Technical error during account creation"
- **Cause**: Temporary system issue
- **Solution**: Wait a few minutes and try again. If the problem persists, contact support.

### Getting Help

If you encounter any issues or have questions:

**Email Support**: support@theflyingbus.com
**Response Time**: Within 24 hours during business days

**Include in your support request**:
- Your email address
- Your child's name (as submitted in the invitation request)
- Description of the issue
- Any error messages you received
